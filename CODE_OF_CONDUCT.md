# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be a respectful experience for
everyone, regardless of career stage, discipline, language, nationality,
gender, disability or technical background.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith and ask clarifying questions before assigning intent.
- Respect the boundary between this GUI and the original `MCMCtreeR` package.
- Credit upstream authors and contributors accurately.
- Keep bug reports reproducible and free of private or sensitive data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing private
information, or deliberately disruptive behaviour are not acceptable. Do not
post security exploits publicly.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any project space
associated with the repository.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer at <zengzichao@sjtu.edu.cn>. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may remove content, close threads or restrict
participation when needed.
